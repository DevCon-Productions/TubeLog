TubeLog Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 15, 2026

What TubeLog Does

TubeLog is a Chrome extension that keeps a personal log of YouTube videos you watch, including the video title, URL, and duration.

Data Collection

TubeLog collects the following information from YouTube pages you visit:

  • Video title
  • Video URL
  • Video duration
  • Date and time watched

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage API (chrome.storage.local). Your data never leaves your browser.

Data Sharing

TubeLog does not:

  • Send any data to external servers
  • Share data with third parties
  • Use analytics or tracking services
  • Collect any personal or identifying information

Permissions

TubeLog requires access to YouTube pages solely to read video titles and durations. It does not access any other websites or browsing data.

Data Deletion

You can delete all stored data at any time by removing the extension from Chrome. You can also clear individual entries from the history page within the extension.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can open an issue at github.com/DevCon-Productions/TubeLog.
